# ShopNova Mobile App & Notifications Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Last Updated:** March 1, 2024
**Document Version:** 2.0

---

## 1. Overview

This document covers features specific to the ShopNova mobile app (iOS and Android), push notification preferences, and troubleshooting for app-related technical issues. For policies that apply equally to the app and the website (returns, payments, shipping), see the relevant dedicated policy document.

For questions not covered here, customers may contact ShopNova support through the app, website chat, or by email at suporte@shopnova.com.br.

---

## 2. App-Exclusive Features

### 2.1 App-Only Deals

Certain promotions are marked "App Exclusive" and can only be redeemed through the mobile app, not the website. These are typically shorter-duration flash sales tied to app engagement campaigns.

### 2.2 Barcode Scanning

The app includes an in-store barcode scanner that looks up a product on ShopNova by scanning its barcode, useful for price comparison. This feature requires camera permission and is not available on the website.

### 2.3 Order Tracking Map

The app shows a live delivery map for orders with an active carrier integration, updating the estimated arrival window as the package moves. This feature is not available for all sellers or all shipping regions — where unavailable, the app falls back to the same status text shown on the website.

---

## 3. Push Notifications

### 3.1 Notification Categories

Push notifications are grouped into three categories, each independently toggled in the app under Settings > Notifications: **Order updates** (dispatch, delivery, delay alerts), **Support messages** (replies from a support teammate), and **Marketing** (sales, personalized recommendations, app-exclusive deals).

### 3.2 Order Update Notifications Cannot Be Fully Disabled

Order update notifications can be muted in-app, but a delivery-day notification is still sent by SMS or email as a fallback, since missing a delivery attempt notification can result in a failed delivery attempt per Section 8.2 of the Shipping & Delivery Policy.

### 3.3 Support Message Notifications

When a support teammate replies to an escalated conversation, a push notification is sent immediately regardless of the customer's marketing notification preference, since this is considered a transactional message tied to an active support case, not marketing.

---

## 4. App Account Sync

### 4.1 Single Account Across Platforms

Logging into the app with the same email used on the website accesses the same account, order history, ShopNova Rewards balance, and saved payment methods — there is no separate "app account."

### 4.2 Offline Behavior

The app caches recent order status for offline viewing, but placing a new order, applying a promo code, or messaging support all require an active internet connection. Cached data shown while offline may be out of date by the time connectivity is restored.

---

## 5. Technical Support for App Issues

### 5.1 App Crashes or Login Failures

App-specific technical issues (crashes, failure to load, login errors not related to a forgotten password) should first be addressed by updating to the latest app version from the relevant app store, since ShopNova does not provide technical support for outdated app versions more than two major versions behind the current release.

### 5.2 Reporting a Bug

A reproducible app bug can be reported from Settings > "Report a Problem," which attaches basic device and app version information automatically. This channel is for app defects, not for order-specific issues, which should go through the normal support channels described in each relevant policy document.

### 5.3 Supported Devices

The ShopNova app supports iOS 15 and later, and Android 10 and later. Devices on unsupported operating system versions can still use the ShopNova website in a mobile browser with full functionality.

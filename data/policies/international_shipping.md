# ShopNova International Shipping Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Last Updated:** March 1, 2024
**Document Version:** 2.0

---

## 1. Overview

Most ShopNova orders ship within Brazil, covered by the Shipping & Delivery Policy. This document covers the additional rules that apply specifically to orders shipped to or from outside Brazil.

For questions not covered here, customers may contact ShopNova support through the app, website chat, or by email at suporte@shopnova.com.br.

---

## 2. Countries ShopNova Ships To

### 2.1 Supported Countries

ShopNova currently ships internationally to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Portugal, Spain, and all European Union member states. Availability for a given product is shown at checkout — not every seller or item is eligible for international shipping.

### 2.2 Restricted Countries and Items

ShopNova does not ship to countries under active international trade sanctions. Certain product categories (perishables, hazardous materials, and some electronics with battery restrictions) cannot be shipped internationally regardless of destination, consistent with the Non-Returnable Items exclusions in the Return & Refund Policy.

---

## 3. Customs, Duties, and Taxes

### 3.1 Who Pays Duties

International orders may be subject to import duties, customs fees, and local taxes charged by the destination country, which are the customer's responsibility and are not included in the ShopNova order total. These fees are collected by the customs authority or carrier at the time of delivery, not by ShopNova at checkout.

### 3.2 Estimating Duties

An estimated duties range is shown at checkout for supported countries based on the item's declared value and category, but the final amount charged by customs can differ from the estimate. ShopNova is not able to guarantee the exact duty amount in advance.

### 3.3 Refused Customs Charges

If a customer refuses to pay customs charges and the package is returned to ShopNova by the carrier, the product cost is refunded to the original payment method, but the original outbound shipping fee and any return shipping charged by the carrier are non-refundable.

---

## 4. Currency and Pricing

### 4.1 Displayed Currency

Prices are displayed in the customer's local currency based on their shipping address, converted from Brazilian Real (BRL) at checkout using the exchange rate current at the time of purchase. The exact amount charged may vary slightly from the displayed estimate due to the customer's card issuer applying its own conversion rate.

### 4.2 Refund Currency

Refunds for international orders are issued in the same currency the original charge was made in, at the exchange rate applicable at the time of the refund — this may result in a refunded amount that differs slightly from the original charge due to exchange rate movement between purchase and refund.

---

## 5. International Delivery Times

### 5.1 Estimated Delivery Windows

- **United States, Canada**: 10–18 business days
- **United Kingdom, Portugal, Spain**: 8–15 business days
- **Other EU countries**: 10–20 business days

These estimates begin from dispatch, not from purchase, and do not include potential delays for customs clearance, which is outside ShopNova's control.

### 5.2 Late Delivery Compensation Does Not Apply Internationally

The 10% Late Delivery Compensation voucher described in the Shipping & Delivery Policy applies only to domestic Brazilian orders. International delivery estimates are provided as a guide, not a guaranteed window, due to the added variability of customs processing in each destination country.

---

## 6. International Returns

### 6.1 Return Eligibility

International orders follow the same category-specific return rules as domestic orders (see the Return & Refund Policy), with the standard 30-day window still measured from confirmed delivery.

### 6.2 Return Shipping Costs

International return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the item is defective or damaged on arrival, in which case ShopNova covers return shipping and reimburses any customs fees paid on the original outbound shipment once the defect or damage is confirmed.

### 6.3 Extended Processing Time

International returns take longer to process than domestic ones due to customs transit time on the return shipment. Customers should expect 15–25 business days from drop-off to refund confirmation for an international return, compared to the standard domestic timeline.

# ShopNova Marketplace & Seller Terms

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Last Updated:** March 1, 2024
**Document Version:** 3.0

---

## 1. Overview

ShopNova operates as a marketplace: some items are sold directly by ShopNova, and others by independent third-party sellers using the ShopNova platform for listing, payment, and fulfilment. This document explains how the marketplace model affects customers — seller obligations, dispute resolution, ratings, and ShopNova's role as intermediary.

For questions not covered here, customers may contact ShopNova support through the app, website chat, or by email at suporte@shopnova.com.br.

---

## 2. Identifying the Seller

### 2.1 Seller Information on Listings

Every product page displays whether the item is "Sold by ShopNova" or "Sold by [Seller Name], fulfilled by ShopNova" or "Sold and fulfilled by [Seller Name]." This distinction determines who is responsible for stock, packaging, and (for seller-fulfilled orders) shipping.

### 2.2 Contacting a Seller Directly

Customers can message a third-party seller directly through "My Orders" > "Contact Seller" for pre-purchase questions or order-specific issues. Sellers are required to respond within 2 business days. If a seller does not respond, ShopNova support can be asked to step in.

---

## 3. Seller Obligations

### 3.1 Registration and Verification

Sellers must register a valid business or individual tax ID (CNPJ or CPF), a return address within Brazil, and pass a product-category eligibility check before listing certain restricted categories (electronics, health and beauty, food).

### 3.2 Listing Accuracy

Sellers are contractually required to list accurate product descriptions, images, and stock levels. A listing found to misrepresent a product (wrong material, incorrect dimensions, stock photos not matching the actual item) can be removed by ShopNova and repeat violations can result in seller account suspension.

### 3.3 Counterfeit and Prohibited Items

ShopNova prohibits counterfeit goods, items infringing intellectual property, and any product on Brazil's restricted-goods list. Reported counterfeit listings are investigated within 3 business days; confirmed counterfeit orders are fully refunded to the customer regardless of the standard return window, and the seller's account is suspended pending review.

### 3.4 Fulfilment Time Commitments

Seller-fulfilled orders must be dispatched within the handling time shown on the listing (typically 1–3 business days). A seller who repeatedly dispatches late may be downgraded in search ranking or, for persistent violations, suspended.

---

## 4. Seller-Caused Delays and Cancellations

### 4.1 Seller-Initiated Cancellations

If a seller cancels an order (out of stock, unable to fulfil), the customer receives a full automatic refund to the original payment method within 1 business day, per Section 6.4 of the Payments & Cancellations FAQ. No action is required from the customer.

### 4.2 Compensation for Seller Delay

A seller-fulfilled order delayed beyond the estimated delivery window is covered by the same Late Delivery Compensation policy described in the Shipping & Delivery Policy (Section 6.2) — the compensation comes from ShopNova, not the individual seller, so the customer does not need to negotiate with the seller directly.

---

## 5. Ratings and Reviews

### 5.1 Rating a Seller

After a seller-fulfilled order is marked delivered, the customer can rate the seller (1–5 stars) separately from rating the product itself, covering packaging, dispatch speed, and communication.

### 5.2 Seller Rating Thresholds

A seller whose rolling 90-day average rating falls below 3.5 stars is flagged for review by ShopNova's marketplace team. Sellers below 3.0 stars for two consecutive review periods may be suspended from the platform.

### 5.3 Removing a Review

ShopNova removes a seller review only if it contains personal information, harassment, or is proven fraudulent (posted by an account with no matching order). A negative review reflecting a genuine service problem is not removed at the seller's request.

---

## 6. Disputes Between Buyer and Seller

### 6.1 When ShopNova Intervenes

If a customer and a seller cannot resolve an issue directly through "Contact Seller" within 5 business days, either party can escalate to ShopNova's marketplace resolution team, which reviews order records, messages, and (for damage or wrong-item disputes) photo evidence submitted by the customer.

### 6.2 ShopNova's Role as Intermediary

For seller-fulfilled orders, ShopNova acts as an intermediary rather than the seller of record, but guarantees the same refund and return protections described in the Return & Refund Policy and Warranty & Damaged Goods Policy regardless of which party — ShopNova or the seller — technically fulfilled the order. Customers are never required to accept a lesser remedy because an item was seller-fulfilled.

### 6.3 Decision Timeline

Marketplace disputes are typically resolved within 5 business days of escalation. ShopNova's decision on a marketplace dispute is final for the purposes of the platform, though it does not affect a customer's separate rights under Brazilian consumer protection law.

---

## 7. Seller Payouts (Informational)

Seller payouts are processed on a rolling basis after the order's return window has closed, net of ShopNova's marketplace commission. This section is provided for transparency; customers do not need to take any action related to seller payouts.

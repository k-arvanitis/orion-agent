# ShopNova Rewards Loyalty Program

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Last Updated:** March 1, 2024
**Document Version:** 2.0

---

## 1. Overview

ShopNova Rewards is the platform's free loyalty program. Customers earn points on every purchase, redeem points for discounts, and unlock tier benefits as their spending grows. This document covers enrollment, point earning and redemption, tiers, expiry, and referral bonuses.

For questions not covered here, customers may contact ShopNova support through the app, website chat, or by email at suporte@shopnova.com.br.

---

## 2. Enrollment

### 2.1 Automatic Enrollment

Every customer with a ShopNova account is automatically enrolled in ShopNova Rewards at the Bronze tier. There is no signup fee and no minimum purchase required to join.

### 2.2 Viewing Your Balance

Points balance and tier status are visible under "My Rewards" in the app or website account menu, alongside a running history of points earned and redeemed.

---

## 3. Earning Points

### 3.1 Standard Earning Rate

Customers earn 1 point for every R$1 spent on completed orders. Points are credited once an order reaches "delivered" status, not at the time of purchase, so that refunded or canceled orders do not generate points.

### 3.2 Bonus Earning Categories

Certain categories earn points at a higher rate during promotional periods, shown on the product page and in the shopping cart before checkout. Bonus rates never apply retroactively to orders placed before the promotion started.

### 3.3 Points on Partial Refunds

If an order is partially refunded (for example, one item returned from a multi-item order), points are recalculated based on the final paid amount, and any excess points already credited are deducted from the customer's balance.

---

## 4. Redeeming Points

### 4.1 Redemption Rate

Points are redeemed at checkout in blocks of 100 points for a R$5 discount. Partial redemptions below 100 points are not possible, and points cannot be redeemed for cash.

### 4.2 Combining Points with Promo Codes

Points redemption can be combined with one promo code per order, applied in the order: promo code discount first, then points, then any applicable shipping discount. Points cannot be combined with another points-based redemption.

### 4.3 Redemption on Returned Orders

If an order paid for partly with redeemed points is returned, ShopNova refunds the cash portion to the original payment method and restores the redeemed points to the customer's balance rather than issuing cash for the points.

---

## 5. Tiers

### 5.1 Tier Thresholds

Tier status is based on points earned (not redeemed) in the trailing 12 months:

- **Bronze**: 0–999 points earned in the trailing 12 months
- **Silver**: 1,000–4,999 points
- **Gold**: 5,000+ points

### 5.2 Tier Benefits

- **Bronze**: standard point earning rate, birthday discount code
- **Silver**: free standard shipping on orders over R$50 (versus the normal R$150 threshold), early access to sale events by 24 hours
- **Gold**: free standard shipping on all orders, early access to sale events by 48 hours, priority support queue placement

### 5.3 Tier Review and Downgrade

Tier status is reviewed on the first day of each calendar quarter. A customer whose trailing-12-month points fall below their current tier's threshold is moved down one tier at the next review, not immediately — there is always at least one full quarter's notice before a downgrade takes effect, shown as a banner in "My Rewards."

---

## 6. Point Expiry

Points expire 24 months after the date they were earned, tracked on a first-earned-first-expired basis. An expiry warning is sent by email and app notification 30 days before any points are due to expire. Expired points cannot be reinstated.

---

## 7. Referral Bonuses

### 7.1 How Referrals Work

Each customer has a unique referral code, shared via "My Rewards" > "Invite a Friend." When a new customer signs up with a referral code and completes their first order of R$50 or more, both the referrer and the new customer receive 200 bonus points.

### 7.2 Referral Limits

A customer can earn referral bonuses for up to 20 successful referrals per calendar year. Referral bonus points follow the same 24-month expiry as regular points and count toward tier thresholds.

### 7.3 Referral Fraud

Referral codes may not be used to refer existing customers, duplicate accounts, or accounts created solely to generate bonus points. ShopNova reserves the right to reverse referral bonuses and, in cases of repeated abuse, suspend the referral feature for the accounts involved.

---

## 8. Program Changes and Termination

ShopNova may modify point-earning rates, tier thresholds, or benefits with 30 days' advance notice by email. If ShopNova Rewards is discontinued entirely, customers will have 90 days from the announcement to redeem any remaining point balance before it is forfeited.

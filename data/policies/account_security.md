# ShopNova Account & Security Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Last Updated:** March 1, 2024
**Document Version:** 3.0

---

## 1. Overview

This document covers account creation, login and password management, two-factor authentication, device and session management, and how customers can exercise data privacy rights over their ShopNova account.

For questions not covered here, customers may contact ShopNova support through the app, website chat, or by email at suporte@shopnova.com.br.

---

## 2. Account Creation

### 2.1 Required Information

Creating a ShopNova account requires a valid email address, a phone number for order notifications, and a CPF (for Brazilian customers) or equivalent tax identification for customers ordering from outside Brazil. A password must be at least 10 characters with at least one number and one letter.

### 2.2 One Account Per Customer

ShopNova permits one account per individual. Multiple accounts registered to the same person for the purpose of claiming duplicate promotions or referral bonuses may be merged or suspended under Section 7.3 of the Loyalty Program policy.

### 2.3 Guest Checkout

Customers may complete a purchase without creating an account. Guest orders can be tracked using the order confirmation email and order number, but do not earn ShopNova Rewards points and cannot be viewed later in "My Orders" unless the customer creates an account with the same email address within 30 days.

---

## 3. Login and Password Management

### 3.1 Password Reset

A forgotten password can be reset from the login screen via "Forgot Password," which sends a reset link to the account's registered email, valid for 60 minutes. If the reset link expires, the customer must request a new one.

### 3.2 Password Requirements and Reuse

New passwords must differ from the previous 5 passwords used on the account. ShopNova does not enforce a mandatory password expiry, but recommends changing passwords periodically and immediately after using a shared or public device.

### 3.3 Account Lockout

After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, the account is temporarily locked for 15 minutes as a brute-force protection measure. The lockout does not affect any pending orders or scheduled deliveries.

---

## 4. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

### 4.1 Enabling 2FA

Two-factor authentication is optional and can be enabled under Account Settings > Security. ShopNova supports SMS-based codes and authenticator apps (TOTP). Once enabled, login requires both the password and the second factor.

### 4.2 Losing Access to a Second Factor

A customer who loses access to their 2FA method (lost phone, uninstalled authenticator app) can regain account access through an identity verification process requiring a government ID photo and proof of the registered email or phone number, reviewed within 2 business days.

### 4.3 Recommended for High-Value Accounts

ShopNova recommends enabling 2FA for any account with a saved payment method or Gold tier status, since these accounts represent a higher-value target for account takeover attempts.

---

## 5. Device and Session Management

### 5.1 Active Sessions

Account Settings > "Active Sessions" lists every device currently logged into the account, with the device type, approximate location, and last-active time. Any session can be remotely signed out from this screen.

### 5.2 New Device Notifications

A login from a device or location not previously associated with the account triggers an email notification. This is informational — the login is not blocked automatically, but the customer is encouraged to sign out the session immediately if it was not them.

### 5.3 Automatic Sign-Out

Sessions are automatically signed out after 90 days of inactivity on that device, or immediately if the account password is changed, which signs out every other active session as a safety measure.

## 6. Data Privacy Rights

### 6.1 Data ShopNova Collects

ShopNova collects account information, order and payment history, and browsing behavior on the platform to personalize recommendations and process orders. Full details are in the Privacy Policy, available at shopnova.com.br/privacy.

### 6.2 Requesting Your Data

Customers may request a full export of their personal data (orders, account details, support conversation history) from Account Settings > "Download My Data." Exports are prepared within 5 business days and made available for download for 7 days before the link expires.

### 6.3 Account Deletion

Requesting account deletion under Account Settings > "Delete Account" permanently removes personal account details after a 14-day grace period (during which the request can be canceled). Order history required for tax and accounting purposes is retained in anonymized form as required by Brazilian consumer protection law, even after account deletion.

### 6.4 Marketing Communication Preferences

Email and push notification preferences for marketing (as distinct from transactional messages like order updates) can be managed independently under Account Settings > "Notifications." Opting out of marketing communications does not affect transactional order notifications.

---

## 7. Reporting a Compromised Account

A customer who suspects their account has been compromised (unrecognized orders, changed account details they did not make) should immediately reset their password and contact ShopNova support. Orders placed by an unauthorized party before the compromise is reported are handled under the Unauthorised Charges provisions in the Payments & Cancellations FAQ.

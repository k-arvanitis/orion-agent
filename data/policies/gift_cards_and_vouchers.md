# ShopNova Gift Cards & Vouchers Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Last Updated:** March 1, 2024
**Document Version:** 2.0

---

## 1. Overview

This document covers ShopNova gift cards (purchased by a customer for themselves or as a gift) and vouchers (issued by ShopNova as store credit, for example from a refund or a promotional campaign). Both are distinct from the points-based ShopNova Rewards program described separately.

For questions not covered here, customers may contact ShopNova support through the app, website chat, or by email at suporte@shopnova.com.br.

---

## 2. Gift Cards

### 2.1 Purchasing a Gift Card

Digital gift cards can be purchased from shopnova.com.br/gift-cards in fixed denominations of R$25, R$50, R$100, R$200, or R$500. Payment for a gift card purchase can be made by any accepted payment method except another gift card or voucher.

### 2.2 Delivery

Digital gift cards are delivered by email, either to the purchaser or directly to a recipient's email address if specified at checkout, typically within 15 minutes of purchase confirmation.

### 2.3 Redeeming a Gift Card

A gift card code is entered at checkout under "Apply Gift Card or Voucher." The card's balance is applied to the order total before any other payment method is charged. If the gift card balance exceeds the order total, the remaining balance stays on the card for future use.

### 2.4 Gift Card Expiry

Gift cards do not expire, in compliance with Brazilian consumer protection regulations on prepaid credit instruments.

### 2.5 Lost or Stolen Gift Card Codes

A gift card code that has not yet been redeemed can be deactivated and reissued if the purchaser reports it lost or stolen and can provide the original purchase confirmation email. A gift card already redeemed (even partially) cannot be reissued.

---

## 3. Vouchers

### 3.1 How Vouchers Are Issued

Vouchers are issued automatically by ShopNova in three situations: as store credit for a refund the customer chose to receive as a voucher instead of cash, as the 10% late delivery compensation described in the Shipping & Delivery Policy, and as promotional codes distributed during marketing campaigns.

### 3.2 Voucher Expiry

Unlike gift cards, vouchers do expire. Refund-issued vouchers are valid for 12 months from issue. Late delivery compensation vouchers are valid for 90 days from issue. Promotional vouchers carry the expiry date shown at the time they were issued, typically 30 to 90 days.

### 3.3 Voucher Value and Partial Use

A voucher can be used for less than its full value in a single order; the remaining balance stays available until the voucher's expiry date. A voucher cannot be redeemed for cash or transferred to another account.

---

## 4. Combining Gift Cards, Vouchers, and Promo Codes

### 4.1 Stacking Rules

One gift card, one voucher, and one promo code can all be applied to the same order, in that order: promo code discount is calculated first, then the voucher is applied, then any remaining balance is covered by a gift card, with any leftover charged to the selected payment method.

### 4.2 Refunds on Orders Paid with Gift Cards or Vouchers

If an order paid partly or fully with a gift card or voucher is returned, the portion paid by gift card or voucher is refunded back to a new voucher (not to the original gift card, and not as cash), matching the handling described in Section 7.4 of the Return & Refund Policy.

---

## 5. Gift Card and Voucher Fraud

ShopNova monitors for gift card and voucher fraud, including codes obtained through unauthorized account access or resale outside ShopNova's own gifting flow. A gift card or voucher found to have been fraudulently obtained is deactivated, and any order in which it was used may be placed on hold pending review.
